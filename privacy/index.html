This policy explains what information Stow Pro Plumbing handles when you visit proplumbingstow.com or call (866) 310-6010.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address, a description of your plumbing problem and anything else you choose to tell the dispatcher. We use that information to arrange and follow up on service.

We do not take payment card numbers over the dispatch line.

This website

This site has no forms, no accounts and no comment sections, so we do not collect personal information through it. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and some pages show a Google Maps embed. Those services may receive your IP address and device details when the content loads, under Google's own privacy policy.

How information is shared

We share job details, such as your address and the problem description, with the plumber arranged to help you. We do not share your information for unrelated purposes, and you can ask us to delete call details we hold by emailing contact@proplumbingstow.com.

Children

This site and our phone line are for adults arranging plumbing service. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions can go to contact@proplumbingstow.com or by phone at (866) 310-6010.
