The county matters because permits, sewer service and inspections are handled locally. Kent sits in Portage County and runs plumbing permits through its own building division, while the other towns are in Summit County. Each page below explains the rules for that town.

What changes from one town to the next

A plumbing emergency feels the same in every town, but the questions that follow it do not. Who supplies your water decides who to call about a main break. Who permits plumbing decides where a repair permit comes from. The table shows the differences we put on each town page.

Rules vary by job, so ask the building office for your town before work starts. If you are not sure which town an address belongs to, the dispatcher can sort it out when you call, and the contact page lists the 24/7 line and our Darrow Road address.

Cuyahoga Falls runs its own well-fed water utility, has some of the oldest homes we cover and keeps sanitary and storm sewers separate, so each season brings its own kind of plumbing call.
