What to have ready when you call

  • 1The address where the plumbing problem is, with the closest cross street.
  • 2A short description of what you see, hear or smell, and whether water is still running.
  • 3Whether you have already shut off the main valve or a fixture valve.
  • 4A phone number where the plumber can reach you.
  • 5Anything that changes safety: standing water near outlets, a gas smell or a sewage odor.

If the problem is in the street or at the city water meter, the City of Stow lists 330-689-5700 for water main break emergencies. For a sewer main backup, Summit County Environmental Services answers at 330-926-2400, with an after-hours line at ext. 1. For problems inside your property, call us on (855) 575-2890.
