Sewer backup in a storm: who owns the blockage

Sewer service in Munroe Falls comes from the Summit County sanitary sewer department. The county's backup page says backups caused by extreme weather, private lateral obstructions or non-flushable items are not a justifiable claim. It does not say who owns the lateral, so ask the county where its responsibility ends.

That makes the first hour of a backup a sorting job. A plumber runs a drain camera down the cleanout to see whether the stoppage is in your own lateral or out in the main. If it is in the main, call the county's after-hours line, 330-926-2400 ext. 1. If it is on your side, sewer line cleaning with a cable or jetter is the usual fix, and the camera footage shows what you are dealing with.

Hardware that matters

Sump pumps, backwater valves and drainage on low lots

Not every wet basement is a sewer problem. Groundwater and surface runoff can seep through the footing or the slab, and that is a job for a sump pump and sometimes for basement waterproofing. A sump pit should discharge to the yard or a storm drain, never to the sanitary sewer.

A backwater valve is different. It sits in the sewer line and closes when flow reverses, which is the exact failure that filled basements here in 2014.
