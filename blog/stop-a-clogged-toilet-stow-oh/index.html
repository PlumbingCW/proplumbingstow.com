How to Stop a Clogged Toilet

To stop a clogged toilet in Stow, OH, first stop the water so it cannot overflow: lift the tank lid and push the flapper closed, or turn the small valve behind the toilet clockwise. Then plunge with a flange plunger using slow, firm strokes. A clogged toilet is a fixture where waste or paper has lodged in the built-in trap, blocking the drain.

If plunging fails after a few tries, or other drains in the house are acting up too, stop and call a plumber. A toilet that backs up along with a shower or sink often points to the main line, not the toilet.

Decide first

Toilet problem or house problem?

Likely a toilet clog

  • Only this toilet is slow or blocked
  • Water rises when flushed but drains in minutes after plunging
  • Other sinks and the tub drain normally
  • Someone flushed extra paper or a wipe

Likely a main line problem

  • Two or more fixtures back up together
  • Water gurgles in the tub when you flush
  • A floor drain or basement fixture bubbles
  • The toilet clogs again right after clearing

Sewer backups in Stow

Stow homes connect to Summit County sanitary sewer, not a city system. The county's backup line is 330-926-2400, and the after-hours line is 330-926-2400 ext. 1. Summit County's sewer backup page names non-flushable items as a cause it does not treat as a justifiable claim.

That means a toilet clog made of wipes or paper towels is usually a house problem. If several drains back up together, a plumber can find out whether the blockage is in your own lateral or in the county main. Call the plumber first, and call the county if the plumber finds the main is the cause.

The method

How to clear a clogged toilet, step by step

1

Stop the water

Lift the tank lid and push the rubber flapper down so no more water enters the bowl. If the bowl is rising, turn the valve behind the toilet clockwise.

2

Protect the floor

Lay towels or newspaper around the base and put on rubber gloves. A bucket nearby helps if you need to bail water.

3

Use a flange plunger

Seat the rubber flange inside the drain opening and press down gently first to push out air. Then plunge with firm, steady strokes for about 20 seconds.

4

Test with a small flush

If the water drains, let the tank refill, then flush once. Do not repeat a full flush while the bowl is still high.

5

Try a toilet auger

If the plunger fails, feed a closet auger into the drain, crank slowly and pull out the blockage. Keep the cranking gentle to avoid scratching the porcelain.

Choose the right plunger

A cup plunger, flat on the bottom, is for sinks and tubs. A flange plunger has an extra rubber sleeve that fits into the toilet drain. Using the wrong one wastes effort and can splash dirty water.

Under the porcelain

What is happening inside the bowl

Every toilet has a curved channel molded into the base, called the trap. It holds a seal of water that blocks sewer gas, and it is the narrowest part of the path, usually around two inches wide. Paper and waste have to squeeze through that bend, so a small mass can stick there even when the drain beyond it is clear.

Plunging works by pushing water back and forth against the plug until it breaks apart. A closet auger does the same job mechanically. If neither moves it, the blockage may be a solid object, such as a toy or a brush head, and the toilet may need to be lifted off the floor.

Skip these

Mistakes that make a clog worse

Flushing again and again

Each flush adds water to a blocked bowl. If the bowl is still high, wait and plunge instead.

Pouring chemical cleaners

Caustic products can sit in a blocked bowl, splash a person who plunges, and damage the wax ring or the finish.

Using a wire coat hanger

A hanger scratches porcelain and can push the clog deeper. A closet auger has a protective sleeve for this reason.

Ignoring the second sign

A bubbling shower or a gurgling sink after a toilet clog means the blockage is further down the line.

Know the limit

When it is time to call a plumber

Stop and call if you see these

Call when plunging fails after several tries, when the toilet clogs again within a day, or when water leaks from the base. Call right away if sewage appears in a tub, shower or floor drain, because that points to the main line.

A plumber can pull the toilet and check for a lodged object, replace worn parts through toilet repair, or clear the pipe behind it with drain cleaning.
